TTShip privacy policy

Effective 4 October 2026.

Who uses TTShip

TTShip is a personal tool. Only its owner signs in to it, and only with the owner's own Google account and YouTube channels.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, TTShip uses Google's YouTube APIs to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channels and set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails;
  • read and update the owner's own videos on those channels;
  • read YouTube Analytics figures (such as views and watch time) for the owner's own videos.

How the data is stored

Sign-in tokens and the analytics figures are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent anywhere except back to Google's APIs.

Sharing

TTShip does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone. It does not use the data for advertising.

TTShip's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services.
